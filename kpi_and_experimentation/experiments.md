### Product: Instagram

### User Journeys
* The onboarding journey on Instagram is very simple if you already have a Facebook account. You just click a button or link and are instantly onboarded.
* Reels – In my view, this is the most successful feature on Instagram. It allows users to upload videos of their moments easily, without requiring any video editing skills.
* Stories – You can share the reels you just uploaded to your stories as well. This is an amazing feature if you want to increase the visibility of your content to other users on Instagram.

### Experiments
* Light Theme Experiment – Instagram currently defaults to a dark theme. I’m curious to see how users would react to a light theme. I’d create two groups: T1 and T2. T1 would use the light theme, while T2 remains on the dark theme. The groups would be split 50/50. This could affect metrics related to acquiring new users, as some users may prefer a light interface.

* Follower/Following Privacy Experiment – Currently, if you accept a follower request, that person can see your followers and the accounts you follow. A 50/50 split test could be run to hide this information for some users. This experiment could impact user retention.

* Different Reactions on Posts – Currently, Instagram only allows one reaction (like) on posts. It might be more engaging to allow users to react with different emotions, similar to Facebook. This could also improve user retention.
